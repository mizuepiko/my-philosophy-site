<h2 style="font-size: 24px;">Mizue Osanai</h2>

Art & Research

My practice explores the boundaries of structural abstraction through an engagement with material possibilities. Moving beyond traditional frameworks, I work with soft and pliable media—such as thread and variable surfaces—not merely as tools of representation, but through their own structural logic. By examining the tactile realities of material, my practice seeks to reframe our perception of space, structure, and physical presence.
